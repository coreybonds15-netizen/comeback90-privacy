Comeback 90 Privacy Policy

Effective October 5, 2026

Comeback 90 has no accounts, no analytics, and no ads. What you enter stays on your iPhone and in your own private iCloud. No one else receives it. That includes the developer.

What the app keeps

  • Your Why. The sentence you write, and your Why photo if you add one.
  • Daily photos. The photo you add each day to show you showed up.
  • Your setup answers. Your persona answers (where you are starting from, and anything else you said is true for you), your age range, your equipment, the joints that give you trouble, your session length, and your wake time.
  • Two safety flags. Whether you checked anything on the "Before we start" screen, and whether you said a doctor cleared you. The app never saves which items you checked.
  • Dose day information. If you tell the app you take a weekly medication, it keeps the weekday you chose for your reminder, and anything you log on dose day. It never keeps a drug name or a dose amount.
  • Your training log. Sessions, soreness answers, walks, and week commits.

Where it lives

On your iPhone. If iCloud is on for your Apple account, a private backup is also kept in your own iCloud, so your data comes back when you change phones.

Apple stores that backup inside your iCloud account. The developer has no access to it and cannot see it.

What is shared

Nothing. No third party receives any of your data. The app has no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no accounts. Nothing is sold, because nothing is collected.

Permissions

  • Photos. The app sees only the photos you pick.
  • Notifications. One reminder a day, created on your phone. Nothing is sent from a server.

Deleting your data

Delete the app to remove everything it keeps on your iPhone.

To remove the iCloud backup, open the Settings app, tap your name, tap iCloud, open Manage Account Storage, choose Comeback 90, and delete its data.

Age

Comeback 90 is for adults 18 and older.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted on this page with a new date.
